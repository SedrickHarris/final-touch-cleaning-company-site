## Hold until owners confirm facts

Check `docs/site-os/owner-confirmed-facts.md` first. Anything listed there is already confirmed.

- Cost, rate per square foot, and estimate posts: pricing is unconfirmed. A factors-only version ("What Affects the Cost of Post-Construction Cleaning") is safe, with no numbers.
- Insurance and certification posts: "licensed and insured" is general only, with no numbers. OSHA 10 is owner-confirmed (2026-11-17) but no certificate number or other detail may be added.
- Health hazard and health code posts: these are medical and regulatory claims and need real sources.
- Contract, lease, and payment deduction posts: these read as legal advice. Cover only from a cleaning-scope angle.
- HVAC duct cleaning and hazardous materials: likely outside scope. Write a "what post-construction cleaning does and does not cover" post instead.
- Restaurant kitchens: scope is front-of-house only.
