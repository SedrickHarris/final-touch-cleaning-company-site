## Operations (from the owners' blog answers)

- Scott and Nicole do about 85% of the physical cleaning themselves.
- Their interest in construction cleanup began when they helped Nicole's father build and clean up several of his homes.
- They mostly clean commercial tenant improvements and remodels across Southern Nevada: restaurants, a coffee shop, a wine tasting lounge, retail spaces, and large offices.
- Every project has an individualized scope agreed with the general contractor, set during a walkthrough.
- They also do rough cleans, including debris removal when agreed.
- Process: top to bottom, HEPA-filtered vacuuming, microfiber cloths, loose-dust removal before wet cleaning, floors last. Photos and videos are taken before starting each job.
- Substantial paint or grout mess: they give the general contractor two options, the responsible trade returns or the owners clean it for an extra charge.
- Grout haze timing: generally clean no sooner than day 3 and no later than day 10 after the tile is set, following the tile and grout manufacturer's guidance.
- They cannot guarantee that no dust appears after the clean (HVAC vents, filters, returning trades, propped doors, ceiling tile gaps).
- Biohazards and hazardous materials are not cleaned; those need a specialist.
- A window cleaning company from their BNI chapter handled interior windows and a glass conference wall on one larger office project.
